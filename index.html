Why it keeps happening

It's not that you're too much

Your body learned to read the rush as love. And the anxiety isn't the enemy.

When uncertainty feels unbearable, we bond fast to make it stop. We fall for someone's potential before we've seen their reality, and by the time the truth shows up, we're too deep to leave.

Reading another book doesn't change this, because the pattern doesn't live in your head. It lives in your nervous system. So that's where we work: you learn to hear what your body is telling you, before your mind builds a future.

The goal isn't to never feel anxious again. Some of that will always be there when you care about someone. The goal is to notice it, check in with yourself, and still choose your truth, even on the days you can't follow the dating "rules".

Discernment is free. Heartbreak is expensive.

Questions

Good to know

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching. We work with what is happening in your dating life now, through your body and your choices.

It works best if you already have some ways to calm yourself when things get hard. If you're carrying severe trauma, such as sexual assault or abuse, and haven't yet had support with it, please start with a trauma-informed therapist. Coaching can come alongside that, or after it, and I'm happy to talk it through on the free call.

Do I need to be dating someone right now?

No. Whether you're single, swiping, or a few dates in, we work with where you are.

What does "somatic" mean?

It means working with the body, not only thoughts. Anxious attachment shows up as a racing heart, a tight chest, the urge to text right now. We learn to notice that and settle it, so you can choose instead of react.

Where does it happen?

Online, on video calls, so you can join from anywhere. We pick a weekly time that works for both of us.

What happens on the free Reset Call?

30 minutes to talk about what's been happening for you and what you want instead. If it feels like a fit, I'll tell you how we'd work together. If not, no pressure at all.
