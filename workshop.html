About

Hey, I'm Jen

I'm a somatic sex and relationship coach, and I've been where you are. I used to confuse intensity with safety, and give my heart away early because I couldn't sit with not knowing.

Somatic work changed that for me. I still feel anxious sometimes. The difference is that I know how to come back to myself, check in, and stay with myself while being with someone else. I practise it every day, in my own dating life.

I believe choosing love shouldn't mean losing yourself. My job is to help you say what you need, hold your boundaries, and let the right person meet you there.
